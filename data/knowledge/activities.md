# Activity and situation guide

Each section suggests catalog moods, genres, target values and a TuneScout ranking mode for a common listening situation. Ranking modes: balanced (default), genre-first, mood-first, energy-focused.

## Activity: studying, homework, reading, coding
Suggested catalog moods: focused, chill.
Suggested catalog genres: lofi, ambient, classical, jazz.
Target energy about 0.35 to 0.45; valence about 0.55; acoustic sound usually preferred. Avoid lyrics-heavy, high-energy tracks.
Suggested ranking mode: energy-focused, because steady low intensity matters more than the exact genre.

## Activity: workout, gym, running, training
Suggested catalog moods: intense, euphoric, confident.
Suggested catalog genres: edm, pop, rock, hip-hop, metal.
Target energy about 0.9 to 0.95; valence 0.6 or higher for motivation; produced (not acoustic) sound.
Suggested ranking mode: energy-focused.

## Activity: party, pregame, dancing, celebration
Suggested catalog moods: euphoric, happy, confident.
Suggested catalog genres: edm, pop, hip-hop, reggae.
Target energy about 0.8 to 0.95; valence about 0.8; produced sound.
Suggested ranking mode: balanced or mood-first.

## Activity: sleep, winding down, meditation, yoga
Suggested catalog moods: peaceful, chill.
Suggested catalog genres: ambient, classical, folk.
Target energy about 0.1 to 0.3; valence about 0.4 to 0.6; acoustic sound preferred.
Suggested ranking mode: energy-focused.

## Activity: road trip, night drive, commuting
Suggested catalog moods: moody, happy, nostalgic, uplifting.
Suggested catalog genres: synthwave, indie pop, pop, country, rock.
Target energy about 0.6 to 0.8; valence about 0.5 to 0.8.
Suggested ranking mode: balanced. For a night drive, synthwave with the moody label fits best.

## Activity: dinner, date night, cooking
Suggested catalog moods: romantic, relaxed.
Suggested catalog genres: r&b, jazz.
Target energy about 0.4 to 0.55; valence about 0.65; medium to high acousticness.
Suggested ranking mode: mood-first.

## Activity: rainy day, feeling down, reflecting
Suggested catalog moods: melancholic, nostalgic, peaceful.
Suggested catalog genres: folk, lofi, classical, country.
Target energy about 0.25 to 0.45; valence about 0.3 to 0.5; acoustic sound preferred.
Suggested ranking mode: mood-first. If the user wants to be cheered up instead, use uplifting or happy moods with valence 0.75 or higher.

## Activity: working, background music, coffee shop
Suggested catalog moods: relaxed, chill, focused.
Suggested catalog genres: jazz, lofi, r&b.
Target energy about 0.35 to 0.5; valence about 0.65.
Suggested ranking mode: balanced.

## How to choose a ranking mode
genre-first: the user names one genre and wants to stick to it, for example "only metal" or "nothing but country".
mood-first: the user describes a feeling and is open to any genre, for example "something happy, any style".
energy-focused: the user describes an activity where intensity matters most, such as studying, sleeping or working out.
balanced: everything else, or a request that mixes a genre, a mood and an energy level.
